<p>每期内容基于当日 X 上的公开讨论，所有链接指向原帖，事实核对参考官方来源。跳过旧闻和重复信息，只收录过去一天内的新发展。</p>
<h3>更新时间</h3>
<p>每天约 08:00（北京时间）发布，中文版和英文版同步上线。</p>

<h3>What is this</h3>
<p>Code Echo is a daily bilingual digest of high-signal AI coding tool, model, and product discussions on X (formerly Twitter). Each issue curates ~10 highlights covering new features, model updates, community reactions, and engineering practices across Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, Devin, Grok Bot, OpenCode, Pi, and more.</p>
<h3>Method</h3>
<p>Each issue is sourced from public discussions on X. All links point to original posts. Facts are checked against official sources. Old news and duplicates are skipped — only developments from the past day are included.</p>
<h3>Update schedule</h3>
<p>Published daily around 08:00 Beijing time, with both Chinese and English versions going live simultaneously.</p>
<h3>Languages</h3>
<p>Each issue has both Chinese and English versions. Use the language toggle (EN / 中文) in the top-right corner to switch between them.</p>
